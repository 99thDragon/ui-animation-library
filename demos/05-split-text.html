The mask is the whole trick

Each line of text sits inside a wrapper with overflow hidden. The line starts translated 110% down — completely hidden — then slides to zero with a power4-out ease. Stagger the lines by 80 milliseconds and the paragraph appears to be typed by light.

No SplitText plugin needed

This version measures word positions to find natural line breaks, then rebuilds the element with masked line wrappers. Same visual result as the paid plugin, and you learn how it actually works.
