Dealt into a deck

Scroll. Each card sticks a little lower than the last, so the previous card's top edge stays visible while the new one covers it. Buried cards shrink and dim — that's the depth cue.

01 sticky

The stack is
pure CSS

`position: sticky` with an index-based `top` does the layout. No scroll library required.

02 offset

Each one sits
18px lower

The offset is what leaves a readable edge for every card already in the deck.

03 depth

Buried cards
shrink & dim

Scale is driven by how far a card has been pinned past its own stick point — so it tracks the scroll exactly.

04 last

The final card
never buries

Nothing comes to cover it, so it holds full scale and opacity. From flowfest.co.uk and kravt.eu.
